NEARBY TRANSPORT · SALAK TINGGI

KLIA Transit, Close to JumStay

Salak Tinggi ERL station is approximately a 10-minute walk from B&B By JumStay. It is a convenient starting point for trips to KLIA, Putrajaya & Cyberjaya, and Kuala Lumpur. Please check the walking route and train timetable before setting off.

Getting around from JumStay

For Putrajaya, take KLIA Transit from Salak Tinggi to Putrajaya & Cyberjaya, then continue by connecting bus or Grab. For Kuala Lumpur, take KLIA Transit towards Bandar Tasik Selatan or KL Sentral and continue by the relevant MRT, LRT or Monorail. Grab or taxi is the simpler door-to-door alternative. Journey times depend on connections and traffic.
